WHY WE BUILT THIS

Independent hotels have the data, the guests, and the ambition — but they are resource-constrained. SwarmForge gives them a personal team of AI agents working 24/7, while keeping the human firmly in control of every high-impact decision.

Privacy by architecture — your data never trains our models or touches another hotel's tenant.
Encrypted end-to-end — AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, UUID tenant isolation throughout.
Safe Mode — strip PII from any document before AI ever sees it. Your choice, always.
Full audit trail — every agent action logged. You always know what ran, when, and why.
Human-in-the-loop — no financial or public-facing action executes without your explicit approval.
