Weekly Opportunities

A soft night or missed add-on, spotted early enough to become a goal the GM owns.

A weekly scan looks for a soft night or a missed add-on while there is still time to act. Each idea can become a goal the GM owns — not a post-mortem at the end of the month, but a move made while the week can still change.

WhatsApp is the backbone

When an opportunity becomes guest-facing — an upsell, a package, a nudge — it goes out as an AI-drafted WhatsApp message, sent by a person. The thread closes after checkout, so guests are never messaged past their stay.

AI embedded with Grok & Claude

The routed intelligence layer scans bookings and the week ahead, spots the soft night early enough to matter, and proposes the goal with its reasoning attached. The GM owns the goal; the AI drafts the work.

What it finds

Soft nights

Quiet dates visible early enough to fill, not explain away later.

Missed add-ons

Guests who would have bought more, spotted before checkout.

Goals, not alerts

Each idea becomes something the GM owns, with work drafted behind it.

Measured, not promised

Opportunities are proposals with outcomes tracked — answered guests, caught nights, accepted offers — so the hotel sees what actually happened, not what was predicted.
