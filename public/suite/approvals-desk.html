The Desk

Approvals Desk

Every proposal with its evidence, risk, and policy fit. The GM approves, edits, or declines.

A guest message, a soft night, or an aging task becomes a proposal. The right person approves it. Approved work is tracked until it is done. That is the whole desk: AI that knows where its authority ends.

WhatsApp is the backbone

Approval links reach the GM on WhatsApp, so decisions happen where the GM already is. Guest-facing messages are drafted by AI and sent by a person — never the other way round.

AI embedded with Grok & Claude

Before anything reaches the desk, the routed intelligence layer checks tasks, hotel policy, and what the guest has already bought. Each proposal carries its evidence, risk, and policy fit, so the decision is fast and explainable.

What waits for a person

  • Anything that spends money or reaches a guest. Costly or sensitive exceptions wait for the GM; routine work flows.
  • Comps and voids. Thresholds are GM-set, so small gestures move and big ones stop for approval.
  • Everything else is tracked. Approved work becomes a task with an owner, status, timing, and an audit trail through completion.

Trust built in

  • Guest identity stays GM-only. Managers work by room number; staff see only the work assigned to them.
  • Policies travel with every proposal. Hotel documents and SOPs are checked before work reaches the queue.
  • An explainable record. Actions, decisions, and task timing stay visible in the hotel's audit trail.
