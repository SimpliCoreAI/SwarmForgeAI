Morning Brief

Arrivals, open tasks, and approvals for the day ahead, in one view.

SwarmForge reads the morning before the GM does. Guest messages, arrivals, open tasks, and approvals waiting for a decision come together in one operating view, so the day starts with what matters instead of a hunt through systems.

WhatsApp is the backbone

Guest messages arrive on WhatsApp overnight and land in the brief's context. Briefs and approval links reach the GM on WhatsApp too, so the morning starts where the GM already is. Nothing about the brief requires the GM to open another inbox.

AI embedded with Grok & Claude

The routed intelligence layer reads the morning — messages, arrivals, the week ahead — and proposes the next move. It never acts alone: a person approves anything that spends money or reaches a guest.
