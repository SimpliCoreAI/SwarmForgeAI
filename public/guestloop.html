GuestLoop · In development

Every stay brings the next one.

GuestLoop is a light marketing CRM for independent hotels and travel agencies. It works on the guests and clients you already have, with their consent, on WhatsApp first. Every reply lands on the SwarmForge desk, and every campaign waits for a person to approve it.

01 / FOR HOTELS

Bring past guests back. See what it earned.

Six things, done well, on top of the stays and conversations the SwarmForge desk already holds.

Know who to contact

  • Guest and client memory. Stays, preferences and consent in one record, built from the desk and your bookings file.
  • Ready-made audiences. Returning guests, lapsed guests, wedding and event enquiries. Ask "who should I contact?" and get a short list with reasons.
  • Enquiry recovery. Unconverted enquiries get a timely, polite follow-up draft instead of going cold.

Reach them, and count it honestly

  • WhatsApp-first campaigns. Email is the fallback. Every campaign is a draft until a person approves it.
  • Lifecycle recipes and slow-date offers. A small set of ready recipes, plus help turning a quiet week or an unsold package into a campaign.
  • A revenue ledger you can trust. Each campaign shows the bookings it matched, labelled matched or extra, so the number is honest.
02 / FOR TRAVEL AGENCIES

The same loop, on client and trip history.

Agencies use GuestLoop to bring travellers back for the next trip, beside the tools they already use.

What agencies get

  • Client memory with consent and trip history
  • Audiences such as past honeymooners, family travellers or festival groups
  • WhatsApp-first campaigns with approval before anything sends
  • Replies and results in one place

What it is not

  • Not an itinerary builder
  • Not a GDS or booking engine
  • Not agency back-office or contracting software
03 / HOW A CAMPAIGN RUNS

AskAI drafts. A person approves. Then it sends.

The same propose, approve, execute and audit rule that runs the SwarmForge desk.

01

Pick

Choose an audience, or ask AskAI who to contact and why.

02

Draft

AskAI drafts the WhatsApp message and an email fallback in your voice.

03

Approve

The GM or agency owner reviews, edits and approves. Nothing sends before that.

04

Count

Replies land on the desk. Matched bookings show up in the revenue ledger.

04 / GUARDRAILS

Market only to people who said yes.

No bought lists. No cold WhatsApp. An ops chat is not marketing consent.

Built in

  • Consent recorded per person and per channel
  • WhatsApp template rules and quiet hours handled by the desk
  • Identity visibility limited by role

What GuestLoop is not

  • Not a PMS
  • Not a booking engine or GDS
  • Not an itinerary builder
  • Not a website builder
