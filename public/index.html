02 / GUESTLOOP

Bring past guests back. See what it earned.

GuestLoop is a light marketing CRM for independent hotels and travel agencies. It works on the guests and clients you already have, with their consent, and every reply lands on the SwarmForge desk.

For hotels

  • Guest and client memory. Stays, preferences and consent in one record, built from the desk and your bookings file.
  • Ready-made audiences. Ask "who should I contact?" and get a short list with reasons.
  • WhatsApp-first campaigns. Email is the fallback. Every campaign is a draft until a person approves it.
  • Enquiry recovery. Unconverted enquiries get a timely, polite follow-up draft instead of going cold.
  • Lifecycle recipes and slow-date offers. Turn a quiet week or an unsold package into a campaign.
  • A revenue ledger you can trust. Bookings are labelled matched or extra, so the number is honest.

For travel agencies

  • The same loop on client and trip history, to bring travellers back for the next trip.
  • Works beside the tools you already use.
  • No bought lists. No cold messages. Only people who said yes.

GuestLoop is not a PMS, booking engine, GDS, itinerary builder or website builder.

03 / STORELOOP

Every order tracked. Every item accounted for.

StoreLoop brings the paper KOTs, minibar vouchers and store registers onto the desk. Service orders get a route, a timer and a charge. Stores get a live count, a variance and an audit trail the GM can read in a minute.

Service orders

  • In-room dining, laundry, minibar, amenities, car bookings, lost and found
  • Each request becomes a desk task with an owner and an SLA timer
  • Charges are sent to your PMS folio, or listed for the desk to post. Your PMS stays the bill.
  • Requests arrive on the same WhatsApp thread. The guest never talks to a second bot.

Stores

  • Stock status and par levels for each store and outlet
  • Book versus physical counts, done on a phone
  • Bar, minibar and consumables variance, with likely causes explained
  • A full audit trail: who issued, counted, adjusted and approved
  • Reorder suggestions the GM approves. StoreLoop proposes, it never orders.
  • A price index of frequently bought items, built from your own purchase entries, with its caveats shown

StoreLoop is not procurement. No purchase orders, no supplier bidding, no POS replacement, no folio.

04 / THE LOOP

AI that knows where its authority ends.

A guest message, a slow week or a stock variance becomes a proposal. The right person approves it. Approved work is tracked until it is done.

01

Signal

A WhatsApp message, the morning brief, a service order or a stock count enters the hotel's context.

02

Suggest

AskAI checks hotel policy, open tasks and history, then drafts the next move: a reply, a campaign or a reorder list.

03

Approve

The GM or manager sees the evidence, the risk and the policy fit, then approves, edits or declines.

04

Track

Approved work becomes a task with an owner, a timer and an audit trail through to completion.
