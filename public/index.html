01 / THE LOOP

AI that knows where its authority ends.

Every useful action follows a visible operating loop. SwarmForge surfaces the issue, assembles the context and routes the decision to the right person.

01

Signal

A guest message, aging approval, room issue or revenue opportunity enters the hotel context.

02

Suggest

The AI team checks current tasks, hotel policies and operational conditions before proposing a next move.

03

Approve

The GM or manager sees the evidence, risk and policy fit—then approves, edits or declines.

04

Track

Approved work becomes a task with an owner, status, timing and an audit trail through completion.

02 / ONE OPERATING VIEW

The day is already busy. The interface shouldn't be.

Each role sees the slice of operations it needs: strategic context for the GM, room-level coordination for managers and focused work for staff.
