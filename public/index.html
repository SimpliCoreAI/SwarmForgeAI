Hospitality-first operator AI

Multi-agent operations for independent hotels.

SwarmForgeAI gives small hospitality teams an AI operating layer for revenue, marketing, guest experience, approvals, and business insight—without hiding what the system is doing. It is built on the PersGraph runtime, which can spin up accountable multi-agent workflows for coordinated development and operations.

Why it matters

Independent hotels have the guest relationships and local character, but not always the tooling or headcount. SwarmForgeAI is meant to act like an accountable AI chief-of-staff layer, not a black box.

What this first version is optimizing for

Visibility

Operators should see what agents did, what they recommend, and what still needs approval.

Trust

High-impact steps should be reviewable rather than silently automated.

Hospitality fit

The first workflows should map to real hotel pain: occupancy, campaigns, labor, guest experience, and ancillary revenue.

Platform path

The hospitality surface is first, not final. The product can later expand into other small-business verticals.
