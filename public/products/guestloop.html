GuestLoop

Every stay brings the next one.

A light travel CRM for independent hotels. It turns past stays into rebookings, fills slow dates, and shows the revenue each campaign produced — without asking the GM to become a marketer.

Why it exists

The OS runs the hotel well, but a GM only feels it on event days and bad days. A tool felt occasionally gets churned. GuestLoop is the layer the hotel feels every week: past guests come back, slow dates fill, and every campaign shows what it earned. The more the hotel uses the OS, the better the marketing gets — stays, spend, tiers, and WhatsApp threads are the data it runs on.

What's in it

Guest book

Every guest and stay in one place, with history the GM can search. Points stay read-only from the hotel's loyalty program.

Enquiry pipeline, with an SLA

Every enquiry has a state and a clock. Fifteen minutes by default, set by the property. Slow replies escalate to the Duty Manager.

White-label capture

The hotel's own enquiry form, on its own site. Guests opt in with separate, unchecked checkboxes per channel and purpose. Submitting an enquiry never requires promotional consent.

Enquiry recovery

An offer with no booking after a day enters recovery. AskAI drafts the follow-up; a person approves it. Nothing auto-sends.

Slow dates

The dates that need filling are visible early enough to act on, with offers the GM can approve.

Lifecycle recipes

Follow-ups that run on the guest's journey, not on a blast list. A guest who went quiet stays quiet — no reply of any kind means no further messages.
How it works, end to end

A guest enquires through the hotel's form. The enquiry enters the pipeline with its SLA clock. The team shares an offer. If there is no booking after a day, recovery starts — AskAI drafts, a person approves. When the guest books, the stay joins the guest book, and the next stay starts from there.

The rules

  • AskAI drafts. A person approves. Nothing reaches a guest on its own.
  • Guest identity stays GM-only by default. Marketing sees enquiries, not the guest list.
  • No CRM-invented folio. GuestLoop reads spend; it never becomes the bill.
  • Consent is per channel and purpose, and STOP is honoured.

What it is not

  • Not a booking engine. It brings the guest back to the hotel's own booking path.
  • Not a mass-email tool. WhatsApp first, email only as a consented fallback.
  • Not a replacement for the OS. It runs on the OS's data and shares its approvals and audit trail.
